**A tension, not a contradiction.** The above-chance detection results in this dossier (Lindsey; Lederman; Macar) and the null (Ferrara c01) were obtained on different models, at different sites and doses, under different prompts. Ferrara's eight small open-weight models at one layer-16 site are not a failed replication of the Claude, Qwen-235B or Gemma-27B results, and those results do not answer Ferrara's null. Read together they bound the claim: detection above chance has been reported in some large models under tuned conditions and not found in small ones under one condition. No study has yet run both under one design.

**Contests shown.** Singh c04, c05 and c06 contest Lindsey c03 and c04 on confounds (prompt-induced reports, judge sensitivity, gaslight controls); the contest flags on those entries point to this.

**Who studied whom.** Lindsey (Anthropic on Claude) and Scaling Monosemanticity (Anthropic on Claude) are developer studies of the developer's own closed models; no outside replication is possible on closed weights. Lederman and Mahowald, Macar, Singh, Ferrara, the prefill study, Goldowsky-Dill and DeLeeuw are independent or mixed teams on open-weight or API models. The assistant-axis paper is by Anthropic authors about other developers' open-weight models (Gemma, Qwen, Llama); its entries carry no developer flag for that reason.
