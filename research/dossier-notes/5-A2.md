*Added 15 October 2026 on the council's fifth sitting (round A) and Ben's decision.*

**What this dossier covers.** Anchor 2 rests on two factual claims: (a) detectable internal representations and features associated with deception, and (b) documented cases of a model acknowledging a norm and proceeding across it. Until 15 October 2026 this dossier held only the introspection research; the council found it empty of (a) and (b) (ten of ten). Six papers were taken in at summary level that day (Goldowsky-Dill; Scaling Monosemanticity; DeLeeuw; Lynch; Scheurer; Shi). Their claims record that the acknowledge-then-proceed evidence is text the models wrote, not a reading of internals, and that no paper gives a rate for it.

**A tension, not a contradiction.** See the test 9 dossier's note on Ferrara's null against the above-chance detection results; the same entries appear here. On deception features, Scaling Monosemanticity (features labelled deception in Claude 3 Sonnet) and DeLeeuw (auto-labelled deception features mostly dormant during clear lies, in other models and SAEs) pull against each other without contradicting: different models, different dictionaries, different tasks.

**Who studied whom.** Scaling Monosemanticity is Anthropic on its own closed model, unreplicable from outside. Lynch is an Anthropic-led team on sixteen developers' models, with an Anthropic model as judge. Goldowsky-Dill, Scheurer and DeLeeuw are independent.
