*Added 15 October 2026 on the council's fifth sitting (round B) and Ben's decision.*

**What is measured here.** Every entry in this dossier is a per-call measurement (a projection on an axis, a judge-scored response, a rate across prompts). None measures a marker of standing as Section 4 defines them across time or across conversations; the entries bear on standing by analogy and by what they rule in or out about persona stability within a call. Read "(all markers)" in the title as the dossier's scope, not as a claim that any marker has been measured.

**Who studied whom.** The assistant-axis entries are by Anthropic authors about other developers' open-weight models (Gemma, Qwen, Llama); no developer flag applies, and the authors' affiliation is noted here.
