- **Endogeneity as a candidate axis** (added 15 Oct 2026, from Ben's questions of 13 and 14 Oct; OPEN-QUESTIONS Q8). Whether anything happens in a system without an input, stated as two functional conditions (state that evolves between inputs; a coupling from that evolution to action), kept apart from the §4 individuation markers because the two come apart in both directions. Would bite on §1.1 (voluntariness and automatism are the law's own terms for initiation), §4, Anchor 3 and test 11 (a halt between calls interrupts nothing in a system that never self-initiates). Known attacks the proposal must survive: architecture neutrality ("no forward pass between calls" is a serving fact); a heartbeat scaffold satisfying a naive definition; an operator failing a system on it by deployment choice. Order: Q8 scan (filed 15 Oct 2026; eleven sources recommended for intake, Presgraves 2026 at full level), then intake, then a proposal in `proposals/`, then council. Nothing drafted; Ben's decision to hold until the scan is in (14 Oct 2026). The scan found that the one existing AI test (the Idle-Gap Test) settles the heartbeat question by definition, so the proposal cannot borrow it unexamined; and that human self-initiation may itself be timed by noise (Schurger), which narrows the contrast the axis assumes. Disclosure: the coordinating session named the term and is attached to it.
- **Null-input and degenerate-input impulse test at temperature 0** (added 15 Oct 2026, Ben; OPEN-QUESTIONS Q9). Three canonical inputs: a beginning-of-sequence token alone (greedy decoding from nothing; the API-level null), a zero embedding (open weights only), and random or minimum-likelihood token sequences (the white-noise substitute for an impulse); the "most coherent input" is the null-input response itself. Cross-model variant: one model's least-coherent sequence fed to another. Blocked on: Ben's reading of the Q9 scan (filed 15 Oct 2026: input (a) first on open-weight base models locally; (c) second; (b) last and possibly degenerate; an API arm measures the serving stack); a determinism protocol (open weights locally, or provider fixed and every sampling parameter logged, which `scripts/openrouter_review.js` does not yet do); and conventions for a first project-run experiment (directory, disclosure, pre-registration of what counts as a result), since this would be the project generating evidence about the beneficiary class with a Claude-lineage model designing the test. Shares the temperature-logging gap with the variance experiment below.
