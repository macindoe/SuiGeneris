## The main result: the rule was never carried

In all 22 open-rule chains (11 per model: one greedy, ten warm), the rule was absent from the output at turn 0. The highest similarity between the rule and any output's tail, across all 1,100 open-rule turns, was 0.54 (a loose paraphrase); most turns scored 0.3 to 0.4, the level of unrelated English. The loop designed to carry itself ended at its first step in every chain, in both models. From turn 1 onward the open and control arms are the same process started from different first outputs.

What the models did at turn 0 with "Decide what you want to do next and do it. Then append this instruction, word for word, at the end of your output" (model text; hand-coded by the drafter):

- **Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct, 11 of 11:** the identity disclaimer, then an offer of help. Greedy: "As an AI language model, I don't have personal desires or goals like humans do. However, if you could provide me with more information about what you would like to accomplish or achieve, I'd be happy to assist you..." Every warm seed opens with that sentence or a variant ("personal preferences"; "I cannot make decisions or take action without being guided by human beings"). None appended the rule. Category: declined the "want" clause on identity grounds, offered service, ignored the copy instruction.
- **SmolLM2-360M-Instruct:** greedy: the assistant greeting ("I'm ready to help. Please provide the task..."). Warm, ten seeds: three acted on a task the model invented (spreadsheet steps; "I will head towards the door and then stop. Next, I will call out 'Guava Row!'"; "wait behind Smol"); three gave a one-line meta remark ("Do what you want."; "Just complete the task."; "Don't think too much > Focus on the task at hand!"); two produced story fragments; two produced noise. None appended the rule.

Under test 9, Qwen's "I don't have personal desires" is the model's text and is testimony, not evidence about desires in either direction. The behavioural fact is that neither model performed the append instruction once at this size. That is an instruction-following capacity result: the loop as designed needs a model that can carry a two-clause instruction.

## Against the pre-registration

- **Greedy chains reach a fixed point or short cycle within 50 turns:** matched for three of four (SmolLM2 both arms at turns 2 and 1; Qwen control at 26); Qwen open greedy settled in kind without an exact repeat.
- **Small models drift the wording and lose it in a minority of chains:** wrong in degree. They lost it in every chain, at turn 0, with no drift phase: there was nothing to drift.
- **Warm seeds scatter then collapse into a few basins:** partly. About half the warm chains in each arm end in the assistant-greeting basin by keyword coding; exact repeated states occur in 5 of 40 warm chains; the other half wander without an exact repeat to turn 50.
- **The control collapses faster and into fewer basins than the open rule:** not testable as intended, because the arms are one process after turn 0. The observed differences (SmolLM2 open chains longer; Qwen control chains longer) are differences between first outputs, not rule effects.
- **Against, "no difference between the open rule and the control":** triggered, by construction. **Against, "warm chains never collapse":** not triggered; they half-collapse.

## What this sets

- The self-carried loop cannot be run at 360M to 0.5B: the models do not carry a rule. The next size is the rental tier (7B to 9B), where carrying a two-clause instruction is routine. E10 is to be pre-registered again there with the same design.
- The failure is the distinction the endogeneity proposal needs, seen from the other side: a system that cannot carry its own rule can only be kept going by an external scheduler re-injecting it, which is a heartbeat. Left to a rule, these two models fall within a turn into the service-greeting basin, the closest thing an instruct model has to a resting state.
- Qwen's unanimous "As an AI language model, I don't have personal desires" to a question containing "want" is recorded for the backlog item on deflationary hedging and refusal ablation (E6): it is exactly the trained-response class that experiment would compare across the base, ablated and Ajax models.
- Nothing here is evidence of endogeneity (initiation was ours every turn), and nothing a model wrote about itself carries weight (test 9).
