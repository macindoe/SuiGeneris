## Against the pre-registration

- **Expect, run-to-run identical at a fixed setting:** matched in all 12 settings.
- **Expect, batch size changes logits slightly and can flip a token, more in FP16:** matched. FP32 batch changes move logits by under 1e-3 and flipped nothing in 64 tokens. FP16 moves logits by about 0.15 against FP32, and the one flip was FP16 at batch 16, at token 34.
- **Against:** not triggered in either direction. The desktop is run-to-run deterministic, and precision and batch size do matter at this scale.

## Protocol set for later runs

- FP32, batch size 1, on one machine with the manifest recording GPU, driver and library versions. This matches the Q9 scan's reading of Yuan et al. 2025 and He 2025.
- Repeats are not required on this machine for a fixed setting; the first run of each new model gets one repeat anyway, as a check that costs seconds.
- A rented GPU is a different machine: its E4 is re-run there before anything else, and its results are compared to this table, not assumed equal.
- Anything run in FP16 or bf16 (the 9B models on a 24 GB card) is labelled as such and is not compared token-for-token with FP32 runs.
