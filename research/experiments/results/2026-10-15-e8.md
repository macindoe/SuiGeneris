Pre-registration: `../PREREG.md`, E8, committed at feecfd8 before the run. Script: `scripts/lab_e8.py` (numpy, CPU, no models). Designed and run by Claude Fable 5.1 (`../CONVENTIONS.md`, disclosure). All four systems were run for 2000 time units from a tiny random initial state with **no input at any time**. "Activity" is the mean distance of the state from its resting point (zero for the network; the fixed point x = 1 for the delay equation). "Sign changes" counts output oscillations in the last fifth of the run. "Sensitivity" is the final distance between two copies whose starting states differed by one part in a million.

## Against the pre-registration

- **Gain 0.8 decays; gain 1.5 sustains with changing output and positive sensitivity:** matched. Above the chaos threshold the network's activity is as high at the end as at the start, its readout keeps crossing zero, and two copies a millionth apart end up 26 units apart, which is the size of the attractor itself.
- **τ = 4 settles; τ = 17 oscillates without settling and is sensitive:** matched. The delay equation with the longer delay never comes to rest and amplifies a millionth into 0.7, about the width of its orbit.
- **Against:** not triggered.

Note on the sensitivity numbers: the log-ratio "Lyapunov estimates" in the raw file (0.0085 and 0.0067 per time unit) are lower bounds, because the distance between copies saturates at the attractor's size long before the run ends. They show positive divergence; they are not measurements of the exponent.

## What this sets

- The definition in `PROGRAMME.md` section 3 has clean passes and clean fails, all artificial: the gain-1.5 network and the τ = 17 delay loop satisfy both conditions (state evolves with no informational input; the evolution produces an output that keeps changing); the gain-0.8 network, the τ = 4 loop and the per-call transformer fail the first condition. "Has intrinsic dynamics" is therefore not a synonym for "is biological" and not a synonym for "is powered".
- Ben's delay intuition (14 Oct) and Mackey and Glass 1977 are borne out in the simplest case: the τ = 4 and τ = 17 systems are the same equation with one number changed, and the longer delay alone turns a system that settles into one that never does.
- The passing systems have no clock and no trigger. A heartbeat would be a third thing: a periodic input to a system that otherwise fails the first condition. That is the distinction the endogeneity proposal needs, and it is now demonstrated rather than asserted.
- These are demonstrations of a definition. They are not evidence about any language model, and nothing here says what a language model with such dynamics would be.
