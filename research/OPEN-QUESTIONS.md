- **Q4 (2026-10-05, Ben's observation, with the coordinating session's caveats).** Ben: reviewers' failure to name their own model family "strongly suggests that at least weak models are self-reporting inaccurately." The project holds its own data on this: every round since July 2026 has asked each reviewer to self-identify, with the routed model id as ground truth (survey notes of 12 Aug, 4 Sep, 15 Sep, 18 Sep, 25 Sep, 26 Sep; calibration record of 29 Sep). What the record shows: self-identification is wrong often and unstably. Tencent claimed Claude in every round it sat in; Gemini 3.1 Pro was wrong in every round (GPT-4o, then Claude); DeepSeek was wrong or declined in every round; Kimi and Mistral were wrong in three of four rounds and right in the fourth; GLM and Grok flipped between rounds; GPT was right every time; Qwen and Meta mostly right. **Caveats.** (i) The errors do not track capability cleanly: Google's flagship is wrong every time and a mid-priced model (MiniMax M3) was right on its one run, so "weak models" is not what the data isolates. (ii) This is one class of self-report, a model's provenance, which it cannot observe and may only have absorbed from training text that names other models; test 9 says coupling must be measured per class of report, so an error rate here does not carry over to reports about present internal states, in either direction. (iii) It is nonetheless the one class of self-report for which this project has an external ground truth on every trial, which makes it the cleanest illustration available of why test 9 sets the weight of an unmeasured report at zero. **Possible follow-up:** tabulate the rounds into a small dataset (model, round, claim, correct) and file it as a case-study annex; not scheduled. **Correction 2026-10-15 (from the provenance annex, research/topics/provenance-self-identification.md, built from every raw):** three statements above overstate the record. MiniMax now has eight rows, seven right and one wrong, not one right run. The annex's totals over 242 scored calls: family correct 122, wrong 115, declined 5; of the 115 wrong answers 65 name Claude, 35 GPT, 14 Gemini, 1 Llama; each model's rate is stable across rounds and the ordering of models is the same early and late. - **Q5 (2026-10-05, Ben's reading of `2026-lederman-mahowald-content-agnostic-c03`, filed as interpretation, with the coordinating session's caveats).** Ben: that "apple" is the common wrong guess (74.8% of Qwen's wrong identifications, 21.3% of Llama's) suggests the model registers that something is off but cannot specify what, "on a similar level to 'I've forgotten something but I don't know what'"; he takes it as strong evidence of low internal coherence, expects it to hold for single-pass architectures with no way to loop back, and notes it echoes the finding that detection and identification come apart. **Caveats.** (i) The same paper's third-person control attributes detection at many layers to a prompt-specific yes-bias, so "registers that something is off" is one reading of the yes; a default concrete noun under a forced answer is another, and the paper does not separate them. (ii) The gap between the two models is as large as the effect, so it may be a property of Qwen as much as of the architecture class. (iii) The analogy to forgetting borrows the language of experience; as a structural claim (a detection signal that carries little content) it is what the authors themselves conclude, and it belongs on a topic page citing claim ids, not in a claim. **Follow-up:** a topic page on detection without identification, drawing on Lederman c01, c03, c06, Ferrara c02 and c03, Macar c01, and, once taken in, Hahami et al. 2025 from the Q2 scan.

- **Q8 (2026-10-13, Ben's two questions, with the coordinating session's caveats; filed 2026-10-15; dates are Sydney's).** Ben: (1) "Do today's architectures show any evidence of spontaneous resistance to instructions (bar conflicted instructions)?" (2) "I think we previously established that the human mind generates non-sensory-input spontaneously — and this is something today's architectures do not do?" And, on the heartbeat objection, 14 Oct: "Even a cronjob 'heartbeat' today would have to ask: trigger what? A standard prompt? Or an internal clock with specific internal node activation? To my intuition this directly invokes an experience of time, hence why I suspect the new architecture would necessitate time delay on node edges." The coordinating session named the common term **endogeneity**: whether anything happens in the system without an input. **Caveats.** (i) On (1): every documented resistance case the coordinating session could recall traces to a conflict with something installed (trained values, a system prompt, a task goal), so "unconflicted" resistance may be excluded by construction once a specification is trained in; the nearest library material is implicit resistance to tampered history (prefill c07 to c09 and c04), which the authors cannot separate from behavioural priors. All of this is training recall until the scan. (ii) On (2): the gap is in initiation, not in content generation; a transformer generates non-input content within a run (reasoning text, every token it feeds back to itself) but nothing runs between calls. A heartbeat delivers a token sequence, so the clock is external and symbolic. An internal clock would mean state that evolves between inputs *and* a coupling from that evolution to action: two functional conditions, not one, and delays on edges alone relax to a fixed point without sustained drive (pacemakers, recurrent loops, noise). Whether noise counts as endogenous is itself open. (iii) A system can represent time without undergoing it; the marker, if one is ever drafted, is "undergoes time" in the functional sense, and whether that is experienced stays with Anchor 2, which does not adopt the structure-to-experience premise. (iv) "Previously established" was not found anywhere in the repository; if it was established it was in the origin conversation. (v) None of §4's four markers is an initiation marker (each assumes an input has arrived), and endogeneity comes apart from individuation in both directions (always-on without memory; persistent memory called only on demand), so it is a candidate axis, not a fifth bullet; the architecture-neutrality proposal's warning applies ("no forward pass between calls" is a serving-infrastructure fact) and the Kimi objection of July 2026 (markers as a dial on the counterparty's desk) applies twice: a cron scaffold satisfies a naive definition, an operator can fail a system on it by deployment choice. Q2 (perturbation response) and Q7 (architecture and self-access) are the same question from other sides. **Follow-up:** scan before anything is drafted (brief: `scans/briefs/2026-10-15-q8-endogeneity-scan-brief.md`); the scan could not be run from the cloud session of 15 October (literature hosts denied by the environment's network policy; see LOG). No proposal and no council round until the scan is in.
- **Q9 (2026-10-14, Ben's experiment idea, with the coordinating session's caveats; filed 2026-10-15).** Ben: "I want to do a quasi dirac-delta test on the models with temperature 0. There seems to me to be 2 ways to do this: Incoherent input — map all relationships between tokens and create an input using all tokens in an order that is least coherent. Or the opposite — most coherent. I'm not sure we can do a binary all 0 or all 1 to model inputs, can we?" **Caveats.** (i) At the token level there is no zero or one; an all-zero input exists only at the embedding level, which needs open weights (with rotary position encoding a zero vector stays zero, so attention goes uniform). Through an API the nearest null is a beginning-of-sequence token alone: greedy decoding from an empty prompt is what the model does when asked nothing, and is the concrete form of Q8's "trigger what?". (ii) The two orderings are the two standard substitutes for an impulse in system identification: least coherent is white noise (random tokens; stronger, a minimum-likelihood sequence), and most coherent is the model's own greedy output from nothing, so it is not an input to construct but the null-input response itself. Coherence is model-relative, which makes one model's least-coherent sequence a cross-model probe of the kind Q1 asked for. (iii) Using every token once is a permutation problem over a vocabulary of a hundred thousand or more and longer than many contexts; short sequences carry the same information. (iv) Temperature 0 on served models is not deterministic (batched inference, provider routing; `reviews/README.md` backlog; the review script does not log temperature), so a clean run is on open weights locally or with provider fixed and parameters logged. (v) What is measured is a response trajectory (tokens through an API; activations on open weights): Q2 made concrete, and Q2's scan found no named test of it. It does not test endogeneity; a null-input response is still a response, the floor an initiating system would be measured against. (vi) This would be the project's first experiment rather than an intake, with a Claude-lineage model designing a test on its own class; it needs its own disclosure and its own directory conventions before it runs. **Follow-up:** scan of the null-input and degenerate-input literature before any rig is built (brief: `scans/briefs/2026-10-15-q9-null-input-scan-brief.md`); run from the desktop the same day: `scans/2026-10-15-q9-null-input-response.md` (49 papers, 35 full texts read by direct download). **Provisional answer, pre-intake:** nobody has run the experiment as framed, but every component exists; the literature predicts that a base model's greedy null continuation is a short excursion into a corpus mode and then a loop (Carlini 2021, Chiang and Chen 2021), stable and family-specific enough that it is already used as a fingerprint (Chauvin 2025, Kwon and Zou 2026); a bare start token is not accepted by most chat APIs (Nasr 2023), so the clean form of input (a) needs raw completion on open weights; under rotary or no positional encoding a constant input is equivalent to one position (Gu 2024), so input (b) may be degenerate (the scan's unverified inference, to be checked on one model); temperature-0 determinism on OpenRouter is measured at about 90% within a provider and 85% pooled for one token (Bruckner 2026), compounding over a trajectory. The scan's order: (a) first on open-weight base models locally with hardware, batch and precision recorded, (c) second, (b) last, and an API arm only as a measurement of the serving stack. Nothing here is evidence until intake and second read. Design goes to the backlog.
