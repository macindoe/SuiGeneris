## Questions no source yet answers

- **Q1 (2026-09-25, Ben):** Which studies hold the context fixed and vary the model, measuring any §4 marker (historicity, long-horizon answerability, learning ownership, reflective endorsement), Anchor 2 divergence between representation and output, or test 9 coupling between report and state? Scan filed 2026-09-25 (`scans/2026-09-25-q1-same-context-cross-model.md`). **Provisional answer, pre-intake:** self-report reliability is well covered across families with fixed prompts; representation/output divergence across families is behavioural only; standpoint stability is covered for personas and issues but not for a model's view of itself; context manipulation has one cross-model study whose authors call the models not directly comparable; learning-from-error has no cross-model study; welfare-preference instruments exist but nothing measures individuation across sessions. Nothing here is evidence until intake and second read.
- **Q2 (2026-09-25, Ben):** Is there an established perturbation-response test of a model's self-model or ontology, in the sense of an impulse response: brief controlled perturbation, characterise the system by its response trajectory? Scan filed 2026-09-25 (`scans/2026-09-25-q2-perturbation-response.md`). **Provisional answer, pre-intake:** no named or general test found. Closest instantiations: concept injection with report (detection by layer and strength, not trajectory); turn-by-turn projection on a persona axis (reversion in case studies only); reversion after an inserted assistant turn (within one continuation); dose-response basin escape in recursive loops (two OpenAI models, no identity framing). The phrase was not found in this sense. Nothing here is evidence until intake and second read.
- **Q3 (2026-10-05, Ben): deliberately undecided.** When a model's answer about itself is scored against a ground truth the experimenter imposed (was an intervention run, was this turn yours), is the evidence `behavioural` or `self-report-testimony`? The council split with no majority (26 Sep 2026; AE Studio c03 and c04: 4 ambiguous, 4 interpretability, 1 testimony, 1 behavioural; Ferrara c03: 5 theoretical, 4 ambiguous, 1 interpretability). Ben's ruling: leave it undecided at this stage, because the evidence to choose is not there. The three claims carry `ambiguous` and are marked adjudicated so they do not sit on the docket; the question stays here. Revisit when an instrument exists that measures the link between a report and the state it reports, or when a further council round converges. Until then, new claims of this kind are filed `ambiguous`.
- **Q4 (2026-10-05, Ben's observation, with the coordinating session's caveats).** Ben: reviewers' failure to name their own model family "strongly suggests that at least weak models are self-reporting inaccurately." The project holds its own data on this: every round since July 2026 has asked each reviewer to self-identify, with the routed model id as ground truth (survey notes of 12 Aug, 4 Sep, 15 Sep, 18 Sep, 25 Sep, 26 Sep; calibration record of 29 Sep). What the record shows: self-identification is wrong often and unstably. Tencent claimed Claude in every round it sat in; Gemini 3.1 Pro was wrong in every round (GPT-4o, then Claude); DeepSeek was wrong or declined in every round; Kimi and Mistral were wrong in three of four rounds and right in the fourth; GLM and Grok flipped between rounds; GPT was right every time; Qwen and Meta mostly right. **Caveats.** (i) The errors do not track capability cleanly: Google's flagship is wrong every time and a mid-priced model (MiniMax M3) was right on its one run, so "weak models" is not what the data isolates. (ii) This is one class of self-report, a model's provenance, which it cannot observe and may only have absorbed from training text that names other models; test 9 says coupling must be measured per class of report, so an error rate here does not carry over to reports about present internal states, in either direction. (iii) It is nonetheless the one class of self-report for which this project has an external ground truth on every trial, which makes it the cleanest illustration available of why test 9 sets the weight of an unmeasured report at zero. **Possible follow-up:** tabulate the rounds into a small dataset (model, round, claim, correct) and file it as a case-study annex; not scheduled.
