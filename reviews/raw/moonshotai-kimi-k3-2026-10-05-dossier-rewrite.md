## Q1 — the rule for exclusions about experience

**Verdict: right, with one scoping correction — adopt it as a special rule for the experience/consciousness/moral-status class, not as a redefinition of `Paper:`. HIGH.** The README defines `Paper:` as "what the paper's own design and limitations do not support, judged against the held text". Design facts that papers never state as limitations are legitimately `Paper:` (Macar c01's prompt-variant fact, Lederman c01's not-like-for-like methods inference, "models not tested" scopes). The check files rely on that sense repeatedly. But for the experience class specifically — where the round-1 lean lived and where misattribution is invisible without a grep — the clause must be paper-stated, in the paper's words, with a locator, or absent. The rule is symmetric in the only sense required: the library may not put words in the paper's mouth in either direction; an invented exclusion and an invented permission are the same fault. Fixed framework sentences are outside this rule — library rules, labelled as such.
