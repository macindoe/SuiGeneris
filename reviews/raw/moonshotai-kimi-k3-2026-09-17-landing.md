## D. The test 9 proposal

**The symmetry clause holds formally, not yet in measurement.** The trained-denial reading does not break the clause, because the clause presumes honesty in neither direction — it subjects both to the same zero default and the same coupling requirement. But the asymmetry migrates: denial-classes and claim-classes of report may differ in *measurable* coupling, and the labs whose denials matter most control the instruments. Unless the validation programme is required to attempt coupling measurement for denial-classes (trained deflation) as for claim-classes, "symmetric" is paper symmetry and the deflationary default wins by instrumentation gap. One clause fixes this.

**"Zero until measured" does not survive quotation as drafted — and has a second hole.** First, "AI self-report is admissible as data" truncates clean; "admissible" is an evidence-law word and will travel alone exactly as "narrowing" did. The fix is structural: put the zero default inside the minimal quotable unit — "AI self-report is data, not evidence, until report–state coupling has been externally measured for the relevant class and system; it is never sufficient..." Second, and sharper: C3 *is* an externally measured coupling between a stated preference and an internal direction. A motivated reader will cite it as "measured coupling" and claim nonzero weight for stated-preference reports today. The proposal's own text supplies the answer — C3 "shows nothing about accuracy in natural conditions" — but the test must carry it: coupling measured under steering establishes steerability, not veracity; weight-conferring coupling must be measured in the conditions in which the report is offered. Without that clause, the test's first real use will be the C3-as-credential move. Third, "externally measured" is ambiguous between *external to the report* and *external to the developer*; given that only developers currently have access, weight-conferring measurements would otherwise come from the most interested party. §3.5's integrity principle supplies the intended reading — independent of the interested party, or independently replicated — and the test should say so.

**The AGENTS.md rule 2 amendment is proportionate, with a vocabulary condition.** The door is narrow — "where a brief asks for it" — and the labelling duty is already in the rule. The real risk is cumulative: briefs are model-drafted too, and a routine ritual of asking manufactures a corpus of testimony in the reviews directory that future rounds half-remember as evidence. The mitigation is to make the filing carry the test's own vocabulary verbatim — "filed as data, labelled testimony, not evidence" — so the zero-weight default attaches at filing time.

**Compatibility.** §0's second error: the zero default plus never-sufficient plus corroboration means no protection can rest on an empty report; the second error is guarded, not reopened. §7.3's "compromised in ways neither party can fully audit": the amendment operationalises that unauditability rather than denying it — weight-by-measured-coupling is what you do *because* the channel can't be audited from inside; the third-party-steering control is the right inclusion. Gemini's keep-it-categorical position has the correct prior (faking is the base mechanism) and the wrong conclusion: a categorical rule makes §7.1 unrevisable on this point, foreclosing a question §0 holds open in the protection direction. The amended test encodes Gemini's prior *as the default* and exits it only on measurement — the right synthesis.
