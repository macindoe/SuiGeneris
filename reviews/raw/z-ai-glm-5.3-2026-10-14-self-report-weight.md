## 1. Decomposition

1. **Target and checkability.** What is the report about (provenance, past action, present state, preference, experience), and is the target checkable independently of the model's internals — held label, log, third-party record — or only via internal measurement?
2. **Elicitation and channel integrity.** Which system; who elicited (developer, researcher, adversarial user); under what intervention (ordinary operation, steering/injection/ablation/fine-tune); prompted or spontaneous; and did the intervention itself alter the reporting channel (the anaesthetic case)?
3. **Validation and controls.** Does a measurement exist for this target–class–system–elicitation triple, and what does it show — accuracy against a held label (checkable targets) or external report–state coupling (non-checkable targets) — under error-mode controls: trained mimicry, sycophancy, confabulation, yes-bias, strategic deception; held-out conditions, adversarial checks, third-person reader controls?
4. **Use.** Screening, corroboration, sole evidence, exclusion, or procedural label? Sufficiency thresholds are use-relative.
5. **Residual weight off-target.** What does the report evidence about things other than its target — training-distribution traces, discriminative ability, response style?

**Why this order:** each question presupposes the one before. SQ1 selects the validation *route* (accuracy vs. coupling — demanding the wrong validator is the first fault the two-error discipline names); SQ2 selects the population any measurement applies to (weight does not transfer across elicitation classes: test 9's steering clause; the prompted-vs-spontaneous gap, ukaisi c06 — AUROC 0.80 prompted, <1% spontaneous flagging); SQ3 is the weight question itself; SQ4 sets how much weight the use needs; SQ5 recovers value in measured-but-wrong reports (Q4: a wrong identity claim is real data about training-text absorption). Error modes and external checks from the brief's starting list are placed, not discarded: kind→SQ1, elicitor/intervention→SQ2, check→SQ1+SQ3, error modes→SQ3, use→SQ4. **Recommendation (LOW):** adopt this ordering.
