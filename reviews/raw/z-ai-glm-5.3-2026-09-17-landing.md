## D — The test 9 proposal

**1. Symmetry vs trained denial: holds.** "Denials of experience are subject to the same rule as claims of it" is procedurally symmetric and substantively per-class: weight attaches "only by externally measured coupling between report and internal state for the relevant class of report and system," so if denials and claims measure differently they weigh differently. The clause never asserts a denial is as honest as a claim; it asserts both are data at weight zero until measured. One caution: the reports that actually arrive are hedged denials, neither clause's pure case; per-class measurement absorbs this, but the round should not let "denials" and "claims" harden into a binary the reports don't come in.

**2. "Zero until measured" in quotation: it will not survive as ordered.** "AI self-report is admissible as data: never sufficient on its own, weighted only by externally measured coupling..." puts the quotable head first. "Admissible" will travel exactly as "narrowing" did. Invert the polarity: lead with "never sufficient evidence, in either direction; its evidential weight is zero until coupling between report and internal state has been externally measured ... — measured, it may carry weight, never alone; unmeasured it is testimony, and must be labelled as such." The quotable fragment becomes the restrictive clause. And drop one term from the apparatus: testimony/data/evidence is three names for two states. **(e)1.**

**3. Rule 2 proportionality: too wide as drafted, for one reason.** "May be filed as data where a brief asks for it" does not import the weight floor. Filed "as data" with no stated weight invites the future move — "it is in the repo as data" — and the project's own process manufactures the aggregate continuously: ten labelled self-reports per round. "Never sufficient on its own" does not clearly close pooling; ten self-reports are still self-report, but "on its own" invites the reading that numbers change the category. The fifth-round occasion was: collect reactions, label unverifiable, never treat as evidence. Say that: "may be filed as labelled testimony of zero evidential weight, individually or in aggregate, where a brief states the purpose for which it asks." With that sentence, proportionate; without it, wider than the occasion. **(e)2, (e)5.**

**4. §0's second error and §7.3: compatible, with the guards.** Zero-until-measured, never-sufficient, labelled testimony, and (if C is adopted) the replicated-evidence trigger for rung 2 mean no protection in the framework rests on self-report alone. §7.3's "compromised in ways neither party can fully audit" is the reason the amended test requires external calibration rather than exclusion; Meta's filed point is the operative argument and I agree with it — the categorical rule was the only line in the document a possible future could not touch, which made §7.1 false at test 9. Two additions the draft needs: a measured report is evidence of an internal *state*, not of its being *experienced* — the state-to-experience inference is Anchor 2's unadopted premise, and the test should say so in a parenthetical the quotation cannot lose **(e)4)**; and "externally measured" should mean externally measured *and independently replicated* — the framework's own bar everywhere else; a single lab's coupling result should not move weight from zero **(e)3)**. One cost, stated plainly: the amendment converts test 9 from a bright line into a standard. Applying it now requires judging whether a measurement exists and is valid, and course-correction tests are supposed to be quick. Nine of ten (filed record) accepted that cost; it is real, and it is why (e)1's ordering is load-bearing — once the test is a standard, its quotable surface must be its restrictions.
