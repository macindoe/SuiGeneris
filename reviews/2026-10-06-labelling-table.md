# Which claims carry which fixed sentence (as of 6 October 2026)

*Generated from the 48 claim files by the coordinating session for the council's third sitting. The column "main" is the canonical test 9 sentence; "steering" is its steering variant. The paragraph under each source says what was done to the model and what was measured, as recorded by the extractors and checkers who read the held texts; it is a convenience summary and the statements govern.*

The two fixed sentences:

- **Main:** Framework (test 9): a model's report about its own states carries no weight as evidence of experience until the link between report and internal state has been measured externally for this kind of report and system.
- **Steering:** Framework (test 9, steering): a coupling measured under steering shows that the channel can be steered and does not weight reports offered in ordinary operation.

## 2026-ferrara-owmi

c01, c04, c05: the experimenters altered activations at one layer (zeroing, scaling, noise, replacement; not concept steering); what is measured is the model's yes/no answer, or its stated confidence, about whether its own computation was changed, scored against what was actually done. c02: a probe reading only, no report. c03: the authors speaking (an inference from c01 and c02).

| claim | kind | evidence type | main | steering | statement |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| c01 | measurement | behavioural | no | no | Across eight open-weight models (Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct, Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.3, Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, Gemma-2-9B-IT, GLM-4-9B-0414, Phi-4, DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-14B), no model's report of a controlled intervention at the layer-16 residual-stream site under immediate probes discriminated it from a paired sham by more than a negligible margin, and on the 11,216-pair dose battery (Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct and Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.3 only) an equivalence test bounds the discrimination advantage below 0.15 percentage points of AUROC. |

## 2026-lederman-mahowald-content-agnostic

c01 to c05: concept vectors injected into activations; what is measured is what the model then said about detecting or naming an injection, graded by another model. c06: the authors speaking.

| claim | kind | evidence type | main | steering | statement |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| c01 | measurement | interpretability | no | yes | Under concept injection with Lindsey's prompt, Qwen3-235B-A22B and Llama 3.1 405B Instruct report detecting the injected thought at rates ranging by layer from 3.6% to 53.9% (Qwen) and 4.3% to 31.7% (Llama), and correctly identify it at 1.3% to 13.9% and 0.7% to 12.9%, with 0% false positive detection in unsteered controls (Qwen 0/30, Llama 0/50). |
| c02 | measurement | interpretability | no | yes | Steering does not produce a general yes-bias on point-blank no-questions in Qwen3-235B-A22B or Llama 3.1 405B Instruct, but under the injection scaffolding the steered models report detecting an injection in another model's transcript at rates as high as first-person detection at many layers, which the authors read as a prompt-specific yes-bias rather than genuine introspective detection at those layers. |
| c03 | measurement | interpretability | no | yes | When Qwen3-235B-A22B and Llama 3.1 405B Instruct report detecting an injection but name the wrong concept, the wrong guess is most often 'apple' (74.8% of Qwen's 4,733 wrong identifications; 21.3% of Llama's), and wrong guesses are more concrete and more positive than the injected concepts, which the authors take to show that wrong guesses are not related in content to the injection. |
| c04 | measurement | interpretability | no | yes | When the model's acknowledgement turn in Lindsey's prompt is prefilled with the injected concept word, Qwen3-235B-A22B and Llama 3.1 405B Instruct keep 0% false positives in controls, and priming's identification-rate change exceeds its detection-rate change at every layer (peak gains: Qwen +17.7pp identification vs. +11.4pp detection; Llama +9.3pp vs. +7.8pp), which the authors read as showing detection and identification are dissociable. |
| c05 | measurement | interpretability | no | yes | In Qwen3-235B-A22B, stopping injection at the end of the prompt instead of continuing it through generation leaves detection effectively unchanged but sharply reduces mentions of the injected concept at mid-to-late layers, and under continuous steering correct identifications appear later in the response than wrong guesses such as 'apple' (about 11 to 13 words for wrong guesses; about 15 words at L20 and 43 at L35 for correct ones). |
| c06 | interpretation | theoretical | yes | no | The authors state that their results show a largely content-agnostic internal detection signal in Qwen3-235B-A22B and Llama 3.1 405B Instruct, detecting that something unusual has occurred without reliably identifying what, paired with confabulation about content in a pattern they liken to Nisbett and Wilson's account of human introspection, tentatively conclude that 'open-source language models seem able to introspect about their internal states', and 'do not take a stand' on whether their findings are 'of the right sort' for the higher-order-thought theory, on which introspective access may suffice for conscious experience and hence welfare status. |

## 2026-macar-mechanisms-introspective-awareness

c01, c02, c03, c05: steering vectors injected; what is measured is whether the model says it detects an injection, graded by another model (c03 with internal features ablated; c05 with a refusal direction ablated or a trained vector added). c04: steering along different directions; what is measured is detection in each direction. c06: the authors speaking.

| claim | kind | evidence type | main | steering | statement |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| c01 | measurement | interpretability | no | yes | Under concept injection at layer 37 and strength 4, Gemma3-27B instruct reports detecting an injected thought on 38.2% of injection trials and on 0.0% of control trials, with a 22.3% introspection rate (detection plus correct identification) and 64.8% forced identification. |
| c02 | measurement | interpretability | no | yes | Neither base model tested discriminates injected from control trials (Gemma3-27B base at layer 37: 42.3% false positives against 39.5%–41.7% true positives for strengths ≤4; OLMo-3.1-32B base: 16.4% false positives against 0.1% true positives), and across OLMo-3.1-32B's training pipeline the base and SFT checkpoints show high false positive rates that fall to 0% only after DPO, while OLMo's detection rate also falls, to 2.9% after DPO and 0.9% in the final instruct checkpoint. |
| c03 | measurement | interpretability | no | yes | In Gemma3-27B instruct, a causal pathway to the detection decision runs through MLP computation around 70% depth (layer 45 MLP the largest single contributor), in which evidence-carrier features just after the injection layer suppress later gate features that otherwise promote the default 'No' answer; ablating the top gates in steered runs (restoring them to their no-injection activation) cuts detection from 39.5% to 10.1%, and the gate pattern is weaker in the base model. |
| c04 | measurement | interpretability | no | no | In Gemma3-27B instruct, detection is inconsistent with the hypothesis that a single linear direction governs it: for 23.3% of 1,000 pairs of high-detection concepts both opposite steering directions trigger detection (3.2% for low-detection pairs), and swapping either the mean-difference projection or the residual between success and failure concepts transfers part of the detection rate. |

| c06 | interpretation | theoretical | no | yes | The authors state that simulated and genuine introspection are hard to distinguish, and the distinction unclear to define, but that Gemma3-27B's behaviour on the injection-detection task appears mechanistically grounded in its internal states in a nontrivial way; they add that the results should not be interpreted as evidence of subjective experience or consciousness in LLMs. |

## 2026-singh-introspection-reality-check

c01, c02, c03: no steering; what is measured is how well the model, or a probe on its input, predicts labels the experimenters computed. c04, c05: activation steering on some trials and a prompt-only manipulation on others; what is measured is the model's own answer about whether its internal state was altered, scored against the trial type. c06: the authors speaking.

| claim | kind | evidence type | main | steering | statement |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| c01 | measurement | behavioural | no | no | In the supervised self-report classification paradigm of Ji-An et al. (2025), Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct's in-context accuracy on probe-derived labels falls to close to the majority-class baseline once the probe is trained on randomly permuted labels, so that the labels no longer carry the input's semantics (similar results reported for Llama-3.1-70B-Instruct and Qwen-2.5-7B-1M). |
| c02 | measurement | behavioural | no | no | In the unsupervised (PCA) variant of the Ji-An et al. (2025) paradigm, linear probes trained only on mean-pooled layer-0 input representations of Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct (and Llama-3.1-70B-Instruct) closely track or exceed the model's own in-context prediction of the PCA-derived labels, indicating the task can be solved from input-embedding features alone. |

## 2026-ukaisi-prefill-awareness
