Four recommendations: seven written questions about records as a condition of Commonwealth procurement of agentic AI, through the DTA's procurement guidance and AI impact assessment, with an agency-held tamper-evident copy of the record; the committee to identify the absence of any recording duty on suppliers or operators as a gap in existing law, and a risk-scoped recording requirement to be considered for the 2027 Australian AI standards subject to a pilot; the AI Safety Institute to run that pilot and maintain a short logging standard; definitional and liability provisions drafted as current allocations subject to review. Terms of reference addressed: Commonwealth adoption and accountability measures; adequacy of existing laws; emerging capability and regulators' ability to respond; and, through the annex, barriers to SME adoption. |
